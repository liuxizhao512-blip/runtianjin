Running tips

Warm up before running

Spend 5–10 minutes walking, lifting your knees, rotating your ankles, and doing dynamic stretches to improve flexibility and reduce injury risk.

Cool down after running

Do not stop abruptly. Spend 8–10 minutes stretching your calves, glutes, and hamstrings to help your body recover properly.

Choose the right gear

Select shoes based on the road surface and avoid overly stiff or soft pairs. In summer, choose lightweight breathable gear; in winter, add insulation layers.

Summer safety

Tianjin summers can be hot and humid. Avoid the strongest noon sunlight and try to run in the early morning or late evening while rehydrating regularly.

Winter safety

Cold air and strong wind can affect your comfort. Wear warm layers, protect exposed skin, and be careful on slippery park or coastal paths.

Safety reminders

Night runners should choose well-lit paths, wear reflective gear, and plan a safe route back home before starting.
