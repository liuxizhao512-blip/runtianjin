About this site

Project overview

Run Tianjin is a lightweight city running guide designed for people who want a clearer, more practical way to choose where to run in Tianjin.

The site focuses on local urban contexts: riverside paths, park loops, and coastal routes that feel realistic for everyday runners rather than generic training suggestions.

Who it is for

It is intended for beginners who want a beginner-friendly start, regular runners looking for low-stress routines, and city runners who care about route quality, time of day, and safety considerations.

Why this project exists

The goal is to turn route discovery into a useful decision-making process: what suits your pace, what feels comfortable in your city setting, and which route fits your training goal.
