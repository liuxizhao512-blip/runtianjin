About this site

Project overview

Run Tianjin is a lightweight city running guide designed for people who want a clearer, more practical way to choose where to run in Tianjin.

The site focuses on local urban contexts: riverside paths, park loops, and coastal routes that feel realistic for everyday runners rather than generic training suggestions.

Who it is for

It is intended for beginners who want a beginner-friendly start, regular runners looking for low-stress routines, and city runners who care about route quality, time of day, and safety considerations.

Why this project exists

The goal is to turn route discovery into a useful decision-making process: what suits your pace, what feels comfortable in your city setting, and which route fits your training goal.

What you can do here

  • Browse four curated routes across riverside, park, lake, and coastal areas.
  • Compare distance, surface, best time of day, and difficulty at a glance.
  • Follow a simple 7-day plan that mixes easy mileage, rhythm work, and recovery.
  • Read practical tips on warm-up, hydration, and seasonal safety.

How routes are chosen

Every route is checked for surface quality, lighting, access, and how busy it feels at different times of day. Only paths that work for everyday runners make the list.

Distances are kept realistic—between 3 and 8 km—so a session can fit into a normal weekday without extra travel planning.

Good to know

Route conditions change with weather and seasons. Always check lighting, ice, and crowds before you head out, and adjust your pace to how you feel on the day.
